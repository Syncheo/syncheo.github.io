Moving from Jira to **IBM EWM** is more than moving tickets: teams can also lose rich comments, a workflow that is readable at a glance and, with Confluence, their knowledge base. Syncheo offers **four tools**, each addressing one of these gaps. Use them separately or combined, to **migrate** projects in waves or to **run Jira and EWM side by side**.

**Five combinable packs:** *Connect* (ticket synchronisation), *Essentials* (+ rich comments), *Experience* (comments + workflow, without touching data), *Continuity* (the core of the offer: data, comments and workflow for a migration without disruption) and *Complete* (+ XWiki wiki linked to ELM, to leave Confluence as well).
